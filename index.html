01

Week one — I get inside the business

A 90-minute call, then access to the numbers, ads, email, website, offer, pricing, analytics, calendar, and whatever systems are actually running underneath it. Then I mostly disappear for a while and read.

02

Week two — I hand you one page

What I think is actually happening, what is costing the business the most, what to stop spending time or money on, and what I believe we should build or change first. Then we argue it through until we agree.

03

Weeks three to six — then I build it

A conversion path, an offer, a pricing change, the email system that should have existed two years ago, an automation, an AI workflow — whatever the business is most constrained by, built by me.

04

When it ends, the thing belongs to you

I build in your accounts, under your ownership, in a way you can understand and change after I leave. No dependency, no retainer.
