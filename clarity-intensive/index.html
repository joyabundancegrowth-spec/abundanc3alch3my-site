The work starts before we talk.

Before the first conversation, you send me a bounded set of real evidence: the numbers, offers, acquisition path, site, calendar, responsibilities, and the decision you keep circling.

I read it alone. I look for contradictions, underused assets, missing numbers, and the places where the story about the problem does not match what the business is doing.

That means we do not spend the first call recapping everything you could have sent me. We start where the useful questions begin.

The business is half the read. You are the other half.

The visible business problem and the way you operate can keep reinforcing each other. We look at both.

  • Money + offersWhat sells, where money is made, where it leaks, and which number nobody trusts.
  • Acquisition + conversionWhere attention arrives, where it falls away, and whether the apparent channel problem is actually somewhere else.
  • Founder roleWhat still waits for you, what only appears to require you, and what breaks when you step away.
  • DecisionsHow you decide cleanly, what pressure distorts it, and which decision keeps reopening.
  • CapacityYour best work, fastest drains, sustainable pace, and adrenaline disguised as capacity.
  • Control + avoidanceWhat receives energy instead of the hard move—and what support would remove the correct load.

Two weeks, start to finish.

01

Day 0 · Payment + intake

Your engagement is reserved. I send the evidence checklist immediately. The two-week clock begins when the package is complete.

02

Days 1–3 · The evidence read

I read the business alone and mark the contradictions, symptoms, missing numbers, and possible constraints worth testing.

03

Session 1 · The business

Ninety minutes on the offer, economics, acquisition, conversion, delivery, assets, and who owns what.

04

Days 4–8 · Synthesis

I build a short list of possible constraints and try to disprove each one. The diagnosis has to explain more than one symptom.

05

Session 2 · You, the operator

Ninety minutes on decisions, capacity, avoidance, control, and the operating pattern keeping the business constraint in place.

06

Days 9–12 · Your Clarity Map

You receive one page and a recorded walkthrough: the constraint, the evidence, what needs to stop, the first structural move, and the next 90 days.

One page. No deck. No fifty-item task list.

The Clarity Map shows the reasoning, not just the answer.

  • The constraintOne sentence naming the best-supported problem underneath the symptoms.
  • The symptom stackThe three visible problems it is producing.
  • The evidenceThe business facts and operator patterns supporting the diagnosis.
  • What stopsUp to three things to stop funding, carrying, or pretending will fix it.
  • The first moveOne structural move—not a menu of equally urgent ideas.
  • The next 90 daysThree phases showing the order of operations and what remains unknown.
