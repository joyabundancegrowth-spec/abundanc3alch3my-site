What's actually going on

That conclusion is wrong, and the correction isn't that you're secretly exceptional. It's more boring than that, and more fixable.

Generic advice is written for a generic operator. Whoever wrote it built it on their own wiring, and then removed the part where that mattered. It assumes decisions get made by weighing options over several days. It assumes your energy is a flat, renewable resource you can schedule against.
